Client: Bloom & Brew Café

What product/service is being promoted?
Bloom & Brew Café promotes fresh coffee, handmade pastries, and small flower bouquets for customers who want a relaxing café experience.

Who is the target audience?
The target audience is young adults, college students, remote workers, and local community members who enjoy coffee, pastries, and a cozy place to relax or study.

Which platforms are most suitable?
Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok are the best platforms because they allow Bloom & Brew Café to share photos, short videos, promotions, and seasonal menu items.

Proposed Hashtags:
#BloomAndBrewCafe
#Coffee
#LocalCafe
#FreshPastries
#CozyCafeVibes
#Flowers

Promotional Strategy:
Bloom & Brew Café will use Instagram and TikTok to post short videos of drinks, pastries, and flower arrangements as well as a short video of customers enjoying the peaceful and cozy space. Facebook will be used to share business updates, special offers, and community events to attract local customers.
